# Sample Documents Directory

This folder is designated for dummy test certificates and reference materials used in development and live demonstration.

## Standard Indian Government Certificate Types
1. **Form GST REG-06**: Certificate of Registration issued under Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.
   - Contains: Registration Number (GSTIN), Legal Name, Trade Name, Constitution of Business, Principal Place of Business, Date of Liability, Period of Validity, Details of Approving Authority.
2. **Udyam Registration Certificate**: Issued by Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME).
   - Contains: URN (`UDYAM-XX-00-0000000`), Enterprise Name, Type of Enterprise (Micro/Small/Medium), Major Activity (Manufacturing/Services), NIC Codes.
3. **Certificate of Incorporation (MCA21 Form 11 / SPICe+)**: Issued by Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
   - Contains: CIN (`U...`), Company Name, Date of Incorporation, Registered Office.
4. **PAN Card / e-PAN Letter**: Issued by Income Tax Department, Government of India.
   - Contains: Permanent Account Number, Full Name, Father's Name, Date of Birth/Incorporation.

The platform includes automated generators and extractors for these standard documents.
